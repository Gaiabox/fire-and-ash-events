Our Story

Havana fire. Florida heart.

Fire & Ash is Carlos and Hannah Lopez: husband and wife, parents of two, and the people who will personally answer your inquiry.

The Fire

Carlos was raised on cigar culture.

Born in Havana, Carlos grew up where cigars aren't a product but a ritual: the cut, the light, the unhurried conversation that follows. Bringing that Cuban flare to his Florida community isn't business for him. It's heritage.

Every humidor we stock and every espresso we pull carries that standard.

The Ash... Make That the Warmth

Hannah makes rooms feel like home.

A St. Augustine native, Hannah grew up in Florida hospitality and has a gift for making a crowd of strangers feel like invited guests. She's the reason planners trust us, timelines run smooth, and every event feels effortless.

Together they're also full-time real estate agents and parents of two, which means nothing about running this brand scares them.

Why We Do It
“The best experiences are built around connection. Every build, every event, every interaction exists to make people feel welcomed, connected, and inspired.”
Carlos & Hannah Lopez
Where It's Headed

From serving events to building the machines.

What started as a mobile cigar lounge became an innovation company. Demand for our golf cart humidor kept growing, so we started manufacturing them: patent-pending builds, made to order for clubs, resorts, and collectors across the country.

Two companies, one standard: craftsmanship in every build, hospitality at every event.
