Good to Know

Questions, answered.

How does the Cuban coffee service work?
Espresso is served Cuban style in 2 to 3 ounce pours with elegant disposable cups, included throughout your contracted time on the truck package. No custom coffee orders, just the real thing.
What is included with the cigar service?
A built-in humidor stocked with specialty premium cigars, curated to your taste and budget. Guests can purchase, or you can host a tab with a signed agreement and card authorization.
What should I know when booking the truck?
We need one hour for setup and breakdown, adequate parking, and venue approval for the mobile unit. Our generator hums a little, so we place it thoughtfully.
Are there additional fees?
Florida law requires sales tax on all cigar and coffee sales. Travel is quoted based on your venue location.
Do guests need to know cigars?
Not at all. Our attendants guide first-timers through selection, cutting, and lighting, and can talk shop with lifelong aficionados.
Do you offer partnerships?
Yes. We love building with Florida businesses and country clubs. Email hello@fireandashevents.com.
